How to use

1. Set the applied pressure using the slider or precise input.
2. Press Apply Pressure or Enter to update the simulation.
3. Observe the Bourdon tube and pointer.
4. Select water, mercury or compound mode.
5. Adjust the liquid densities and water height.
6. Observe the calculated mercury height and manometer pressure.

The 3D liquid levels are visually scaled for visibility. The Bourdon mechanism is an educational mechanical model, not a calibrated manufacturing simulation.
